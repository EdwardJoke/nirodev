### 2. Chrome ships JPEG XL
Chrome is shipping JPEG XL, a next-generation image format that promises better compression than WebP, marking a step forward for web media efficiency. [Read more](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/jpeg-xl-in-chrome)

:::brief

Chrome 155 begins shipping JPEG XL decoding support, a next-generation image format offering 30-50% better compression than JPEG with HDR and lossless capabilities.

- The decoder was reimplemented in Rust (jxl-rs) for memory safety instead of the previous C++ implementation.
- SIMD performance was preserved using the stabilized target_feature_11 Rust feature and a jxl_simd abstraction layer.
- Chrome recommends testing both AVIF and JPEG XL, favoring JPEG XL for high-fidelity or lossless photographic compression.

:::

A San Diego pizzeria filed a class action lawsuit on September 30, 2026, alleging Visa, Mastercard, and major banks conspired to artificially inflate merchant credit card transaction fees.

- Defendants include Bank of America, Capital One, Chase, Citibank, and Wells Fargo alongside Visa and Mastercard.

### 10. Nobel Prize in Chemistry awarded
The 2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry has been awarded to Henri B. Kagan and Kenso Soai for their contributions to asymmetric catalysis. [Read more](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/2026/press-release/)

:::brief

Henri B. Kagan and Kenso Soai were awarded the 2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for discovering non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis.

- Kagan's 1986 work enabled greater excess of one mirror-image molecule than previously thought possible.
- Soai achieved in 2003 the first chemical reaction producing only one mirror image, solving a century-old mystery about homochirality in life.
- The Royal Swedish Academy cited their discoveries as providing a solution to how chemical asymmetry emerged spontaneously.
