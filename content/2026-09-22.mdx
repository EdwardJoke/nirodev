### 4. OpenAI's GPT-6 breaks a decades-old cryptographic challenge
In a notable demonstration of AI capabilities, OpenAI's GPT-6 model has successfully cracked the Enigma message known as BGAC, which had resisted all solution attempts since 2005. The achievement marks a rare intersection where large language models have impacted real-world cryptography. [Read more](https://www.cryptocellar.org/bgac/the-mvueh-break.html)

:::brief

OpenAI GPT-6 Astra independently broke the German Army Enigma message MVUEH from 10 July 1941 — a ciphertext that had resisted solution since 2005. The break revealed the left-hand wheel turnover occurs at the 72nd letter, a rare factor complicating earlier attempts.

- MVUEH is 82 letters; plaintext nearly identical to nearby message Nr. 173 (SIPVX, 94 letters) — 12-letter difference from enciphering error
- Recovered key used wheel order 253 instead of 512; plug connections and ring settings differed from daily key
- GPT-6 Astra chose the target autonomously from the Crypto Cellar web page; Carter Leffer only directed it to try

:::
