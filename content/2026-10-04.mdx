### 1. Trump unveils Super Intelligence Force
The White House announced a new federal task force called the Super Intelligence Force, aimed at coordinating AI safety policy and oversight. TechCrunch reports this represents the administration's latest institutional response to growing debates over artificial intelligence regulation and alignment research.


:::brief

President Donald Trump announced the formation of a Super Intelligence Force chaired by national intelligence director Jay Clayton, with vice chairs from the FTC, Undersecretary of War for Research and Engineering, and OPM. The task force has 120 days to produce a report on AI risks and opportunities while preventing overregulation.

- Jay Clayton will serve as the de facto AI czar, having previously said it would be irresponsible for the US to fall behind China.
- Vice chairs include FTC Chair Andrew Ferguson, Emil Michael, and Scott Kupor.
- The task force's charter explicitly says it will prevent "regulatory capture that would stifle innovation."
