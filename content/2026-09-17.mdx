### 1. Huawei speeds up its next-gen AI chip to take on Nvidia
Huawei is accelerating the launch of its Ascend 960DT AI chip to Q1 2027, according to TechCrunch, as it pushes to compete with Nvidia and close China's AI computing gap with the United States. The move signals Beijing's urgency to narrow the hardware deficit ahead of tighter export controls. [Read more](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/17/huawei-plans-q1-2027-launch-of-new-ai-chip-as-it-takes-on-nvidia/)

:::brief

Huawei announced at its Huawei Connect conference that the Ascend 960DT AI chip will launch in Q1 2027, moving up from the previously planned Q3 2027 date. The company is also advancing its Peerium Computing Architecture, which links thousands of chips into large AI systems.

- Huawei spokesperson cited doubled performance for the Ascend 960 series versus prior generations.
- The Atlas 950 SuperCluster can connect up to 256,000 accelerator cards; analyst noted the newly announced SuperPoD uses only 4,096 chips, far below a prior claim of 15,488.
- U.S. semiconductor restrictions on China are not expected to halt the chip push.

:::


### 2. A 100 GW grid push backed by Google, Nvidia, and Anthropic
Google, Nvidia, and Anthropic have joined a coalition asking Emerald AI to identify 100 gigawatts of new grid capacity for data centers, TechCrunch reports. The request underscores how aggressively the AI boom is converging on an energy bottleneck that utilities and regulators are struggling to address. [Read more](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/17/google-nvidia-and-anthropic-want-emerald-ai-to-find-space-on-the-grid-for-more-data-centers/)

:::brief

Emerald AI has formed the AI Energy Management Alliance with Google, Nvidia, Anthropic, and several utilities to let data centers reduce electricity use during peak times in exchange for grid access. The group says demand response could free up to 100 gigawatts of additional data center capacity.

- Emerald AI's software coordinates utility requests with data centers, avoiding diesel generators by shifting or pausing workloads.
- Anthropic, AES, Constellation, National Grid, and NRG Energy are founding members of AEMA.
- Emerald AI raised $150 million in a Series A led by Energize Capital and DCVC last month.

:::


### 3. Global anxiety over AI and jobs
A Pew Research survey of 42,151 people across 37 countries found that a majority view AI as a threat to employment, The Verge reports. The data was collected between February and May 2026, well ahead of recent apocalyptic warnings, suggesting the fear predates the latest wave of alarmist rhetoric around artificial intelligence. [Read more](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/996775/ai-is-feared-globally-as-the-destroyer-of-jobs)

:::brief

A Pew Research survey of over 42,000 people across 37 countries found a majority view AI as a threat to employment, with 34 of 37 countries more likely to predict job losses than new jobs. Concerns about inequality are highest in wealthier nations, and younger adults are increasingly anxious about AI's impact.

- 76% of Australians and South Koreans and 71% of Americans see AI as a job threat.
- Respondents overall were more likely to say AI will widen the income gap than to believe it will reduce it.
- A global median of 41% reported equal concern and excitement; 37% were primarily concerned and 13% primarily excited.
