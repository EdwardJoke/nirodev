### 10. Aleph Alpha Kolibri: How the sovereign German LLM works

Hacker News carried this on 2026-10-03.


:::brief

A blog post examines Aleph Alpha's Kolibri, a 78B-parameter open-weight LLM for German and English built to comply with the EU AI Act. It runs on infrastructure in Germany and Finland and is available under Apache 2.0.

- Kolibri uses a routed Mixture-of-Experts architecture with ~3.5B active parameters per token out of 78B total
- Training data was rephrased using Google Gemma 4 (English), Mistral-NeMo (German), and Qwen3-32B labeled data for quality filtering
- The model scores above every compared model of its size in both German and English according to Aleph Alpha evaluations

:::
