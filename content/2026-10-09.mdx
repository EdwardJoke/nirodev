### 1. Cloudflare acquires Deno

Hacker News carried this on 2026-10-09.


:::brief

Cloudflare announced that the entire Deno team is joining Cloudflare. The company will continue supporting the Deno runtime with monthly bug-fix and security releases for one year, then end its development. Future work will focus on integrating Deno's capabilities into Cloudflare's Workers and celld platform.

- The Deno runtime will receive support for one year before development ends.
- Future development will be directed toward Cloudflare Workers, Durable Objects, and the celld distributed-application platform.
- Users can expect monthly releases with bug fixes and security updates during the support period.

:::

### 2. Remember Orkut? Its founder wants to bring it back

Orkut's founder is now taking aim at algorithms and AI-generated content.


:::brief

Orkut founder Orkut Büyükkökten has called on users to register support for a potential revival of the defunct social network on Orkut.com. He criticized modern social media for algorithm and AI-driven content, saying it erodes authentic human connection. He has not confirmed whether a new version is already in development.

### 3. a16z’s Olivia Moore on the state of consumer AI

Moore sees a huge opportunity in consumer AI, particularly if the industry can tap into revenue streams beyond just subscriptions and API charges.


:::brief

Andreessen Horowitz partner Olivia Moore released a report ranking the top 100 consumer AI apps. While ChatGPT remains dominant, smaller players like Suno and ElevenLabs show staying power. Moore argues the industry should pursue ad-supported revenue models rather than relying on subscriptions, noting only 2.2% of U.S. households currently pay for AI.

- Moore's report identifies half a dozen consumer AI categories that remain completely untapped.
- OpenAI has expanded back toward enterprise, fueling pessimism about consumer AI revenue.
- Most consumer AI revenue comes from subscriptions and token usage, concentrated on the enterprise and prosumer side.

:::
