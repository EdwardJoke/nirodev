The ruling bolsters privacy advocates’ challenges to state‑level internet surveillance mandates. :::brief

A federal judge issued a preliminary injunction blocking Utah's SB 73, an anti-VPN age verification law, finding it likely unconstitutional.

- The law, signed earlier in 2026, required adult websites to block VPN users or verify physical location, making it the first state to target VPNs for age verification.
- The court ruled Utah's approach burdens internet users nationwide, not just Utah residents.
- EFF argued the law demands technical impossibilities and undermines privacy and security worldwide.

### 7. Study reveals extensive data harvesting in connected vehicles
The “Automatic Transmission” project, hosted by Northeastern University, published a data‑privacy study showing how modern cars collect and transmit detailed driver information. The research adds to growing scrutiny over automotive data practices. [Read more](https://automatictransmission.khoury.northeastern.edu/index.html)

:::brief

Researchers at Northeastern University published a data-privacy study of connected vehicles, examining data flows between 21 cars and 30 companion mobile apps from October 2024 to August 2025.

- Both vehicles and their apps send private consumer data to 1st and 3rd party servers over Wi-Fi and cellular connections.
- Data is often shared or sold to undisclosed third parties with no consumer control once it leaves the device.
- Experiments included driving EVs in a Faraday tent to test whether cellular traffic would reroute to Wi-Fi.

### 8. Supabase acquires database platform Turso
Open‑source database provider Supabase announced the acquisition of Turso, a distributed SQLite platform, in a move that expands Supabase’s offerings for edge computing and low‑latency applications. The deal was discussed on Hacker News with significant community engagement. [Read more](https://supabase.com/blog/supabase-is-acquiring-turso)

:::brief

Supabase is acquiring Turso to build infrastructure that scales for AI agents creating millions of databases on demand.

- Supabase will continue with Postgres while Turso continues its SQLite work, giving builders a path from prototyping to production.
- Turso rebuilt SQLite in Rust, enabling a single server to manage millions of databases by loading them on demand and suspending them when idle.
- Glauber Costa and Pekka Enberg will lead the agentic infrastructure effort at Supabase.
